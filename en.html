Biography

Jinrui Sha currently serves as Assistant to the Dean of the School of Cyberspace Security at Nanjing University of Information Science and Technology (NUIST). In 2014, Sha was selected for the Jilin Province team at the Chinese Mathematical Olympiad (CMO) and won a silver medal.

From 2015 to 2019, Sha studied Information Security as an undergraduate at the School of Electronic Information and Electrical Engineering, Shanghai Jiao Tong University. In 2019, Sha began master's studies at the School of Cyber Science and Engineering, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, under the supervision of Associate Professor Lei Fan. In 2021, Sha transitioned to the Ph.D. program under the supervision of Professor Shengli Liu and graduated in 2025. In the same year, Sha joined the School of Cyberspace Security at Nanjing University of Information Science and Technology as a full-time faculty member.

Research & Admissions

Research interests: Public-key cryptography, post-quantum cryptography, and zero-knowledge proofs.

Graduate admissions: I have one opening for a professional master's student in 2027. Students with a relevant background or an interest in theoretical cryptography are welcome to contact me. A strong mathematical background is expected.

Undergraduate students interested in theoretical cryptography are very welcome to join my research! I hope you have a deep interest in this field and are willing to invest time in building a solid theoretical foundation. Research in theoretical cryptography differs from areas such as AI: it typically requires substantial time for learning and careful thought before you can read, understand, and write research papers. If you are hoping for quick research results, this field may not be a good fit for you.

If you are a CMO gold, silver, or bronze medalist and are interested in cryptography, I can offer remote research mentoring.
